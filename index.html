About Us

We are the University of Michigan’s student chapter of the American Society for Biochemistry and Molecular Biology (ASBMB). Our mission is to bring together students who share an interest in biochemistry and molecular biology through social events, guest speaker presentations, professional development opportunities, and hands-on experiences. Whether you're looking to explore career paths, connect with professors and fellow students, or simply meet people who share your interests, ASBMB offers a welcoming community to learn, grow, and get involved.

Mission & Vision

To foster a welcoming community of students passionate about biochemistry and molecular biology through education, research, collaboration, and meaningful connections.

Engaging Experiences

From social gatherings to workshops, guest speakers, and professional development events, we offer ways to learn, connect, and make lasting memories.

Community & Impact

We build an inclusive environment where students form friendships, explore shared interests, support one another, and grow personally and professionally.

How much are dues?

National dues are set at $25 per year, plus an additional $10 for local chapter activities.

Who can be on the board?

Prospective board members must maintain a GPA of 3.5 or higher and be pursuing a major in biology, chemistry, or a related field.
