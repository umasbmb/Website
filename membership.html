Membership

Join our community.

To register as a member of the UMICH ASBMB chapter, candidates must meet specific eligibility criteria. National dues are set at $10 per year. Prospective board members must maintain a GPA of 3.0 or higher and be pursuing a major in biology, chemistry, or a related field. Additionally, freshman students are not eligible to join the society, ensuring that members have a foundational understanding of the discipline and a commitment to academic excellence.
